# 05 — Mechanism: dense curve, channels, regions, pre-touch family

Dense information curve (pre-touch every 0.2 s; flight every 10%), attack- vs
defense-channel decomposition of the in-flight fields, region-wise leave-one-out
(around / first post / second post / center / global), and the strongest known
pre-touch feature family (logistic regression) to close the "flat left side" objection.

**Headline:** two thirds of the total gain accrue in the first flight tenth; ball-2D
reaches 0.729 alone there; channels are mutually redundant; "around the ball" is the
strongest region; best pre-touch family caps at 0.609.
